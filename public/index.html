KNOW THE RULES

Small stakes.
Clear rules.

TON here is Hack A Ton event play currency. This site does not offer blockchain deposits or cash withdrawals.

Piggy Derby

Four equally likely pigs. Pick one before paying. The winner returns 3.8× your entry, including stake; otherwise your entry is lost.

Hog Crossing

Each hop has an independent 80% survival chance. Cars illustrate the result; clicking at a particular time does not change the odds. Cash out after a safe hop, or continue up to eight. Return = entry × 0.95 ÷ 0.8safe hops.

Truffle Trouble

Three mines are hidden among 25 tiles. Reveal up to eight safe tiles and cash out after any safe pick. A mine loses the round. Return = entry × 0.95 ÷ the probability of surviving that many picks.

Settlement

Returns include stake and are rounded down to 0.01 TON. New paid games are refused if the treasury cannot reserve their maximum return. A pending transfer stays pending and is retried with the same payment ID. No game guarantees a profit. Unplayed paid rounds expire after an hour and refund the entry; surviving active road/Mines rounds cash out at expiry.

Fairness

A random server seed is committed as SHA-256 before play and revealed when the round ends. The round details include the client seed, outcomes and generation algorithm. Demo results use local randomness and cannot pay TON.

Private round links

Keep the return URL private: it grants control of that round. Payouts always go to the original payer. Leaderboard nicknames are opt-in; other rounds show an anonymous player.
